## What was found

Archery is confirmed as a traditional Kyrgyz sport with historical roots, and several
named **shooting techniques** recur across sources: **ура атуу** (direct/straight
shooting), **кыңай атуу** (angled shooting), **чалмай атуу** (curved/"whip" shooting),
**топ бузуу** ("breaking the group" — likely a technique for scattering/targeting a
cluster), **кадамак кадоо** (step-shooting), **тоорумай**, and **чертүү** (a
release/flick technique).
