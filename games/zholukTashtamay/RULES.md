# Жоолук таштамай (Zholuk Tashtamay) — Rules

"Жоолук" = handkerchief/cloth, "таштамай" = to drop/throw. A circle-and-handkerchief
tag game — **not** primarily a courtship game.

Status: **core mechanic verified from a Kyrgyz source**; this directly corrects an
assumption in the product spec — see note below.

Source: [azattyk.org — Эстен чыккан элдик оюндар](https://www.azattyk.org/a/1282326.html)

## ⚠️ Correction to the product spec's framing

Spec Section 13 describes this as (in its own hedge) "traditionally a
handkerchief-based social/courtship game" and asks for "character movement, running,
reaction, timing, hiding/placing mechanic, chase, rounds." **The actual documented
mechanic found is a circle tag game structurally similar to what's known in English as
"Duck Duck Goose"** — it is a children's group game, not a courtship game. Build to the
mechanic below, not to a generic chase/courtship framing.

## Setup

- One child (the "walker") is chosen. The rest of the children sit in a row/circle.
  (Source describes one variant where a central child also covers their eyes, but the
  core loop is about the seated row, not that child directly — see UNVERIFIED below.)

## Play

1. The walker circles behind the seated row, secretly placing the jooluk (handkerchief)
   behind one seated child without being noticed.
2. **If the seated child never notices** the handkerchief was placed behind them, play
   simply continues (the walker keeps circling for another attempt / next round).
3. **If the seated child notices**, they immediately get up and **chase the walker**
   around the circle.
4. **If the walker makes it back to the vacated spot before being caught**, the chaser
   becomes the new walker for the next round.
5. **If the walker is caught before reaching the spot**, the source states the chaser
   "hits the sitting child with the [handkerchief]" — read in context this means the
   *original walker*, now caught, suffers this consequence (a light tag/forfeit with
   the cloth) — **the exact attribution of who does what in this final step is a bit
   ambiguous in the retrieved text; treat the precise wording of the forfeit as
   UNVERIFIED**, but the overall shape (walker caught → walker faces a light
   penalty/forfeit and likely stays or restarts as walker) is confirmed.

## UNVERIFIED

- The eyes-covered detail: one phrasing in the source mentions a child covering their
  eyes, which doesn't fully reconcile with the "walker secretly places it while
  circling" framing (in most cousins of this game, the seated players' eyes are closed
  or they face forward/away, not one specific blindfolded child at center). Needs a
  second source to fully resolve the seating/awareness setup.
- Exact forfeit mechanic when the walker is caught.
- Number of players, typical group size.
