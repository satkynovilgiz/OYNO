# Жалал-Абад / Джалал-Абад / Jalal-Abad

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: region (oblast)
- kg: Жалал-Абад облусу · ru: Джалал-Абадская область · en: Jalal-Abad Region

## Facts

- Located in southwestern Kyrgyzstan; total area 33,700 km².
- Administrative center: Jalal-Abad city.
- Borders: Uzbekistan (south/southwest), Talas oblast (north), Naryn oblast
  (east), Osh oblast (southeast).
- Mineral-spring-based resorts began development here during the Soviet
  period; the oblast has ongoing tourism, energy, and mining potential.
- Contains both Arslanbob (world's largest walnut forest) and the
  Sary-Chelek Biosphere Reserve — see their own content files.
