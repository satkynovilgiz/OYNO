# Ош / Ош / Osh

Status: **PARTIALLY VERIFIED**

## Facts

- One of the oldest cities in Central Asia; second largest city in the Kyrgyz
  Republic.
- Located in the eastern part of the Fergana Valley, at the foothills of the
  Alai mountains, near where the Ak-Buura river emerges.
- Sources found in this pass date the ancient settlement to the 4th–5th
  centuries; the exact origin and etymology of the name have multiple
  scholarly interpretations.
