# Чүй / Чуй / Chüy

Status: **VERIFIED** (basic facts only — regional character/tagline not deeply researched)

- Type: region (oblast)
- kg: Чүй облусу · ru: Чуйская область · en: Chüy Region

## Facts

- Administrative center is technically Bishkek, but Bishkek itself is excluded
  from the oblast's territory (separate republican-significance city).
- Major cities within the oblast: Tokmok (oblast center 2003–2007), Kant,
  Kara-Balta.
- Bordered by the Kyrgyz Ala-Too range to the south (separates Chüy valley from
  Talas, Suusamyr, Kochkor valleys — see ala-too.md).
