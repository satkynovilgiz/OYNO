# Нарын / Нарын / Naryn

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: region (oblast)
- kg: Нарын облусу · ru: Нарынская область · en: Naryn Region

## Facts

- Kyrgyzstan's highest-altitude oblast, located in the Inner Tian Shan, central
  part of the country. Borders Ysyk-Kol oblast (east), Chüy oblast (north),
  Jalal-Abad and Osh oblasts (west), China (south).
- 95% of the territory sits above 1,000 m elevation; 70% is covered by
  mountain ranges, including Kakshaal (582 km long — the largest range
  separating China and Kyrgyzstan), At-Bashy, Naryn, Jetim, Moldo-Too, Suusamyr.
- Total area: 45,200 km² — 23% of Kyrgyzstan's territory.
- Home to popular high-altitude lakes: Son-Köl, Kol-Suu, Chatyr-Köl.
