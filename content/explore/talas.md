# Талас / Талас / Talas

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: region (oblast)
- kg: Талас облусу · ru: Таласская область · en: Talas Region

## Facts

- Strongly associated with the "Манас" epic: Talas is where Semetey (Manas's
  son) returns in the epic's second part.
- "Манас" is a trilogy ("Манас", "Семетей", "Сейтек"); it is listed in
  UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity
  and recorded in the Guinness Book of Records as the longest epic in the
  world.
- The epic is also a major ethnographic source describing traditional Kyrgyz
  military campaigns, nomadic life, dwellings, cuisine, clothing, weapons,
  family celebrations, and funeral rites.
