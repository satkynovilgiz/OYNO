## Facts

- Founded in 1878 as "Pishpek" — administrative center moved from Tokmok to
  Pishpek and granted city status on April 29, 1878. Russian settlers had
  established the Alamüdün and Pishpek villages in the area between 1868–1870.
- Renamed "Frunze" in 1926 (Soviet era); renamed "Bishkek" in 1991 upon
  Kyrgyzstan's independence.
- Population reached 18,468 by 1913; today close to ~1 million.
- Capital and largest city of the Kyrgyz Republic.
