# Суусамыр / Суусамыр / Suusamyr

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: nature (high-altitude valley)
- kg: Суусамыр өрөөнү · ru: Суусамырская долина · en: Suusamyr Valley

## Facts

- A distinct physico-geographic region between the Kyrgyz Ala-Too and the
  Suusamyr/Jumgal mountains, at 2,000–3,200 m elevation.
- Roughly 150 km long, up to 45 km wide at its widest; triangular in shape,
  gently hilly terrain.
- Used primarily as summer pasture (жайлоо) — herders bring livestock from
  Chüy, Talas, and Ketmen-Töbö valleys, and even from Kazakhstan's Merke
  district.
