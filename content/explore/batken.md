# Баткен / Баткен / Batken

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: region (oblast)
- kg: Баткен облусу · ru: Баткенская область · en: Batken Region

## Facts

- Located in southwestern Kyrgyzstan; borders Tajikistan (southwest and
  north), Uzbekistan (north), Osh oblast (east).
- Established October 13, 1999, from the Batken, Kadamjay, and Leylek
  districts plus the cities Kyzyl-Kyia and Sulukta, previously part of Osh
  oblast. Administrative center: Batken city (since 2000).
- Youngest of Kyrgyzstan's oblasts.
