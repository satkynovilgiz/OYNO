## Facts

- A major range of the Northern Tian Shan — a large, complex anticline
  running latitudinally from Kyzyl-Ompol (west of Ysyk-Köl) to the city of
  Taraz in Kazakhstan.
- Length: 454 km; width up to 40 km.
- Average height ~3,700 m; highest point is Alamüdün Peak at 4,895 m. Peaks in
  the eastern part of the range exceed 4,000 m.
- Separates the Chüy valley from the Talas, Suusamyr, and Kochkor valleys.
