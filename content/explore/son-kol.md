# Сон-Көл / Сон-Куль / Son-Köl

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: nature (lake), located within Naryn oblast
- kg: Сон-Көл · ru: Сон-Куль · en: Son-Köl

## Facts

- Located in the northwestern part of Naryn oblast, at 3,016 m above sea
  level.
- Surface area ~278 km²; roughly 28 km long, 18 km wide. Maximum depth
  estimated at 14–22 m depending on the source.
- Kyrgyzstan's largest natural freshwater body and second-largest lake in the
  country overall.
- Formed by glacial melt.
- Fed by 18 different streams from the surrounding mountains; only one river
  (also named Son-Köl) flows out, joining the Naryn river.
- Long, cold winters: the lake is solidly frozen from late September through
  May.
