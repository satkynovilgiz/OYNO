# Алай / Алай / Alay

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: nature (high-altitude valley + mountain range), Osh oblast
- kg: Алай өрөөнү · ru: Алайская долина · en: Alay Valley

## Facts

- The Alay Valley sits at ~3,000 m above sea level.
- The Great Alay range's highest point is Lenin Peak (Ленин чокусу),
  7,137 m — the range's summit.
- The Achyk-Tash alpine camp is located at the foot of Lenin Peak.
- The high parts of the Alay and Great Alay ranges support mountaineering and
  tourism; 4 tourist routes operate across the Alay Valley and Great Alay
  range.
