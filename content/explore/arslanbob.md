# Арсланбоб / Арсланбоб / Arslanbob

## Facts

- A village and resort ~690 km from Bishkek, in the Arstanbap valley.
- Home to the world's largest walnut forest — a protected area covering
  ~700,000 hectares (one source's figure; see caveat below), with ~150 plant
  species including walnut, pistachio, almond, wild plum, and pear.
- Some individual walnut trees are reportedly over a thousand years old, each
  yielding roughly 400 kg of walnuts per year (upper figure in sources was
  garbled/inconsistent — see UNVERIFIED).
- Main local livelihoods: tourism and agriculture; peak tourist season is
  June–August.
