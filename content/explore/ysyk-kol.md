## Facts (the lake)

- Endorheic (no outflow) lake; the name means "hot lake" because it does not
  freeze in winter.
- Maximum depth: 702 m; average depth: 278 m.
- Surface area: 6,236 km².
- Located in the northeast of the republic, between the Küngöy Ala-Too and
  Terskey Ala-Too ranges of the Northern Tian Shan, at 1,608 m above sea level.
- Among the world's 30 largest lakes by area; 7th deepest lake in the world;
  11th largest by volume.
- Second most transparent lake in the world after Lake Baikal; slightly
  brackish (mineralization 5.90 ‰).
