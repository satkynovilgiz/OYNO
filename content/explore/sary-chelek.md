# Сары-Челек / Сары-Челек / Sary-Chelek

Status: **VERIFIED**

- Type: nature (lake + biosphere reserve), Jalal-Abad oblast
- kg: Сары-Челек · ru: Сары-Челек · en: Sary-Chelek

## Facts

- Located at ~1,873 m elevation, surrounded by steep forested slopes, peaks,
  and alpine meadows.
- Lake dimensions: 7.5 km long, up to 2.3 km wide, 492 hectares surface area.
  Average depth 98 m, maximum depth 244 m — the second-deepest lake in
  Kyrgyzstan after Ysyk-Köl.
- The Sary-Chelek State Biosphere Reserve was established in 1959 to protect
  and study local flora and fauna — one of Kyrgyzstan's first nature reserves.
- Added to UNESCO's Biosphere Reserve list in 1979 for its unique nature and
  Red-Book-listed species; added to UNESCO's World Heritage List in 2016.
